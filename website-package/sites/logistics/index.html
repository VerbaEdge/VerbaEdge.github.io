Where we are focusing business development

Two corridor groups. One reusable software approach.

The lanes identify commercial research and pilot targets, not live transport routes or confirmed customers.

First pilot focus

France · Morocco · Senegal

Documentation workflows across France–Morocco, France–Senegal and Morocco–Senegal shipments. French-language operations support and separate checks for local document and payment requirements.

Possible user: a forwarder handling multiple country desks and repeated document follow-up.

Next validation track

East Africa

The Northern Corridor around Mombasa, Nairobi and Kampala remains in VerbaEdge's plan. Validate the same handoff and exception workflow locally before expanding implementation.

Possible user: a small logistics operations team coordinating freight milestones.

Complement existing infrastructure.

National systems such as Morocco's PortNet are established trade infrastructure. Our proposed role is customer-side workflow coordination and exception tracking—not official customs submission.

Engagement model

Start with a paid, bounded operations review.

The first deliverable is useful even before a larger platform exists: a documented process map, sample document checklist, exceptions register and clearly estimated next improvement.

Customer data is not needed for the sample.

Initial demonstrations use synthetic examples. Real shipment documents require agreed scope, access controls, privacy terms and retention rules.

  1. 01Workflow discoveryIdentify shipment types, countries, existing systems, document owners and recurring exceptions.
  2. 02Controlled sampleShow the checklist and milestone process with synthetic or properly authorized records.
  3. 03Fixed-scope pilotImplement one approved workflow, acceptance tests and a written operating handoff.
  4. 04Evaluate recurring supportMove to ongoing software support only when the pilot demonstrates enough value.

What VerbaEdge does and does not do

Software engineering for trade operations.

Not a cargo operator

No claim of owning fleets, booking freight as carrier, warehousing, customs brokerage or import/export licensing.

Not a regulated funds intermediary

No transfers, foreign-exchange dealing, custody, escrow, loan origination or bank underwriting service is offered.

No automatic compliance guarantee

Country-specific customs, privacy, contractual and foreign-exchange requirements remain the responsibility of the relevant qualified parties.
